# Future Work & Research Extensions — AU DIC v2.0 Roadmap

The following future research directions and framework extensions are outlined for subsequent benchmark iterations post-v1.0 freeze:

---

## 1. Multi-Lingual & Vernacular Document Support (ADBG v2.0)

- Extend data fabricators to support Indic languages (Hindi, Devanagari, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali) and bilingual academic degree templates.
- Incorporate complex script OCR evaluation metrics.

---

## 2. Advanced Vision-Language Models (VLM Benchmark Suite)

- Benchmarking multimodal LLMs (GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Gemini 1.5 Pro, LLaVA, Florence-2) against AU DIC v1.0.
- Zero-shot vs. Few-shot prompt evaluation across degraded document quality profiles.

---

## 3. Real-World Physical Archive Dataset (AU-DIC-Real v1.0)

- Partnering with university registrar archives to digitize and annotate a complementary 1,000-specimen physical scan benchmark dataset.
- Cross-domain transferability testing between synthetic training (ADBG) and physical evaluation (AU-DIC-Real).
